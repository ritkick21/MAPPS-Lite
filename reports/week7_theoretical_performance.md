# MAPPS-Lite Week 7 Theoretical Performance Analysis

## Objective

This stage converts the Week 7 redox analysis into screening-level battery-performance estimates.

Formal theoretical capacity is calculated from the electron inventory and formula molar mass.

A second conservative capacity estimate applies a utilization penalty to avoid assuming that deep multi-electron delithiation is automatically reversible.

**Voltage values are chemistry-based screening estimates, not DFT calculations or experimental measurements.**

## Capacity Model

The formal gravimetric capacity is calculated as:

**Q = nF / (3.6M)**

where n is the number of electrons transferred per formula unit, F is the Faraday constant, and M is the molar mass.

## Interpretation

The formal theoretical capacity represents the maximum capacity implied by the selected oxidation-state model.

The screening capacity is intentionally more conservative. Materials requiring extraction of several lithium ions per formula unit receive progressively larger utilization penalties.

Specific-energy estimates combine that conservative capacity with a chemistry-based approximate voltage. They should only be used for relative screening within MAPPS-Lite.

Reliable voltage predictions would require electrochemical measurements or first-principles calculations of the energies of multiple lithiated and delithiated phases.
