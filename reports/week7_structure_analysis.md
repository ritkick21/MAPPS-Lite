## Interpretation

The structure score is intended as a screening metric rather than a direct prediction of battery performance.

A high structural score indicates that the material combines several favorable characteristics such as low thermodynamic instability, meaningful lithium content, reasonable Li-site spacing, and physically plausible atomic packing.

Lithium diffusion cannot be established from these descriptors alone. Reliable transport predictions would require methods such as migration-path analysis, nudged elastic band calculations, molecular dynamics, or experimentally measured ionic conductivity.
